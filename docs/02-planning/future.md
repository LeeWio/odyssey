# Future Strategy

This document is the place for the product's longer horizon.

## Expansion Directions

- turn the site into a broader personal product shell
- support more than publishing: capture, organize, reflect, and revisit
- treat content and tools as two sides of the same product
- keep the public-facing narrative distinct from internal workflows
- allow the site to evolve into a platform for multiple personal workstreams, not one fixed homepage

## Likely Future Workstreams

- knowledge capture and personal wiki-style pages
- reading lists, bookmarks, and source tracking
- task and idea intake
- richer profile and identity surfaces
- improved search and discovery across all content types
- stronger content publishing workflows
- analytics or telemetry that help understand usage without adding noise
- projects and case-study pages
- reusable content blocks or templates
- private or semi-private operational dashboards
- cross-linking between posts, notes, references, and modules

## Planning Rules

- do not build future modules as isolated experiments if they are likely to become core
- define ownership and lifecycle before introducing a new content type
- separate prototype, beta, and production states explicitly
- prefer doc-driven decisions for cross-cutting features
- when in doubt, optimize for longevity over short-term novelty

## One-Year View

### Phase 1: Consolidate

- stabilize the current homepage and blog experience
- complete docs coverage for product, design, architecture, development, research, and decisions
- define module boundaries and ownership

### Phase 2: Expand

- introduce one or two future-facing content types such as notes or bookmarks
- improve search and internal discovery
- add clearer content relationships and navigation paths

### Phase 3: Evolve

- support capture, reference, and review workflows
- add template-driven publishing or structured content creation
- evolve the site into a coherent personal operating surface
