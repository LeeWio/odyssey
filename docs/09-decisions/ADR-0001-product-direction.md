# ADR-0001: Product Direction

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Odyssey started as a personal blog but is evolving into a broader personal product. The docs and codebase need a stable product model that can support future expansion without losing identity.

## Decision

Treat Odyssey as a product platform for writing, identity, capture, and future personal workflows, not just as a blog.

## Consequences

- documentation must describe present state and future direction
- new modules must be classified by role and lifecycle
- product decisions should consider long-term reuse and maintainability
