# User Flow

## Primary Flow

1. A visitor lands on the homepage.
2. They understand the identity and tone of the product.
3. They move into the blog or another content surface.
4. They read, explore related modules, or follow links outward.

## Returning User Flow

1. A returning reader checks the latest writing or updates.
2. They use the homepage as a dashboard for what changed.
3. They jump into articles, comments, or module-specific views.

## Future Flow

1. The site may support capture and return loops beyond reading.
2. A user may save, revisit, compare, or synthesize content over time.
3. The product may become a personal knowledge environment rather than a single publication surface.
