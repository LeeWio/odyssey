# Content Strategy

## Current Content Types

- articles and posts
- project or product updates
- personal telemetry and curated signals
- comments and discussion around writing

## Future Content Types

- notes
- references
- bookmarks
- reading logs
- experiments
- micro-updates or short-form captures
- project history and retrospective posts

## Strategy Rules

- every content type needs a reason to exist
- content should be easy to classify, link, and revisit
- the system should support both polished publishing and rough capture
